Mohamed El-Sayed

Project Manager
/ Team Manager

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Define team roles, task ownership, and deliverable accountability to prevent duplicate effort and gaps.
  • ▹Organize team activities into structured tasks, milestones, priorities, and cross-workstream workflows.
  • ▹Monitor progress against agreed milestones, track deliverable completion, and address execution delays.
  • ▹Coordinate dependencies between business, product, and technical streams to ensure objective alignment.
  • ▹Facilitate focused Discovery meetings, establish clear action items and owners, and share progress.
  • ▹Identify workflow obstacles, resolve coordination dependencies, and track execution risks to resolution.
  • ▹Ensure deliverables have defined owners, verify documentation completion, and maintain status visibility.
  • ▹Gather team feedback and resolve workflow bottlenecks to improve coordination efficiency and practices.

Ownership Boundary

Does NOT own day-to-day monitoring of technical task progress (that sits with the Technical Manager).

Mohamed El-Sayed

Product Manager
/ Discovery Owner

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Define and document Discovery objectives, questions, and learning goals.
  • ▹Coordinate with the team to establish the agreed Discovery scope and Guidelines.
  • ▹Use evidence from BD & Market Validation and Technical Feasibility Study to interpret product implications.
  • ▹Translate user needs and evidence into candidate product requirements.
  • ▹Prioritize product requirements and identify/approve Must-have requirements within agreed scope.
  • ▹Ensure proposed deviations/scope changes are brought to team discussion.
  • ▹Ensure agreed outcomes are documented.
  • ▹Use Discovery outputs to support informed recommendation on product's next step.
  • ▹Follow up on Tarek's Discovery Coordinator responsibilities.

Ownership Boundary

Does not replace Technical Owner's responsibility for technical feasibility conclusions. Accountability ≠ unilateral authority to change scope/Guidelines.

Mohamed El-Sayed

Business Development
/ Market Validation Lead

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Research target market, customer segments, market needs, competitors, and alternatives.
  • ▹Investigate customer problems, usage contexts, purchasing considerations.
  • ▹Develop and validate business hypotheses (demand, differentiation, value proposition).
  • ▹Investigate commercial considerations: pricing, cost constraints, business model.
  • ▹Coordinate with factories, suppliers, prospective customers for research/validation.
  • ▹Organize market-facing validation activities and document feedback.
  • ▹Assess commercial implications of technical alternatives.
  • ▹Distinguish verified findings from assumptions.
  • ▹Provide evidence-based recommendations on commercial viability.

Ownership Boundary

Owns market investigation & commercial evidence. PM uses evidence for product decisions. Technical Owner remains responsible for technical feasibility.

Mohamed Arafa

Technical Manager
/ Technical Feasibility Study Lead

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Lead and coordinate technical investigation within agreed Discovery scope.
  • ▹Direct technical research work performed by Arafa, Tarek, Omar.
  • ▹Coordinate technical team's day-to-day work and monitor technical task progress.
  • ▹Consolidate technical evidence and own technical conclusions about feasibility.

  • ▹Clarify technical implications to support product decisions and collective review.

Ownership Boundary

Tarek Hejazi

Discovery Coordinator

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Use agreed Discovery baseline as reference for alignment checks.
  • ▹Observe and document differences between agreed plan/scope and work observed.
  • ▹Report deviations and change requests without independently evaluating severity.
  • ▹Maintain Discovery Coordination & Alignment Log (observations, links).
  • ▹Document outcomes of team discussions and agreed follow-up actions.
  • ▹Coordinate with Tech Owner so investigation stays connected to objectives.

Ownership Boundary

Does NOT track day-to-day technical task progress. Does NOT make technical judgments. Does NOT unilaterally approve scope changes.

Mohamed Arafa, Tarek Hejazi, Omar El-Sersy

Technical Research Contributor

Assigned Tasks

  • ▹Carry out assigned technical research and investigation tasks within agreed Discovery scope.
  • ▹Document relevant technical evidence, findings, limitations, questions from assigned work.
  • ▹Coordinate with Technical Lead on technical work.
  • ▹Provide findings needed for Technical Feasibility Study.

Ownership Boundary

Contributors contribute evidence and perform assigned work, rather than owning final technical conclusions.
